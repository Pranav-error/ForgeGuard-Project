III.

METHODOLOGY

A. System Overview
SIGNAUTH is the AI-powered web application that will
automate the process of verifying hand-written signatures and
detecting forged ones via the deep-learning inference that uses
the Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API.
The system is written in a scalable, modular cloud
application, and each module has the duties of another phase
of the verification pipeline that encompasses explainable
visualisation, authenticity classification as well as input image
preprocessing. The main concept is to provide the signature
analysis with forensic-grade reliability, interpretability, and
privacy. SIGNAUTH relies on AI models to work with the
scanned signature images through heatmap-based reasoning
with resulting outputs that are authentic or fake.

The SIGNAUTH prototype has been tested on the
performance, interpretability, and accuracy using various
datasets. The suggested system combines the feature
extraction with attention modelling by using deep learning
models and Google Gemini 2.5 Flash API to verify
handwritten signatures on a case-by-case basis and in an
explainable manner. It addresses three analytical areas of
prime, which are authenticity detection, feature extraction,
visualisation, and privacy governance. Test Results of
Authenticity Detection Three datasets, GPDS Synthetic,
CEDAR and BHSig260, which consist of authentic and forged
signature samples are the test datasets aimed at evaluating the
accuracy of this system in classifying signatures. Each of such
datasets is subdivided into training and testing subsets in an
80:20 proportion on a regular basis.

D. Analytical Modules
The SIGNAUTH program consists of four separate
analytical modules and each of them is tailored to address a
certain issue related to system integrity and signature analysis.
1. Authenticity Detection: It is a module, which will
identify whether the signatures uploaded are authentic or not.
It relies on a pre-trained CNN ViT hybrid, which has been
prompt-engineered before in Gemini inference. In this aspect
it puts a lot of stress on acquiring local-global trends in order
to be able to keep in check both low-end and skilled forgeries.
2. Feature Extraction Module: To offer the important
biometric feature, the following are the important biometric
features that will be extracted; the writing pressure, the density
of the curves, the smoothness of the contour and the width of
the pen-stroke. They are essential in the identification of
imitations by real handwriting.
