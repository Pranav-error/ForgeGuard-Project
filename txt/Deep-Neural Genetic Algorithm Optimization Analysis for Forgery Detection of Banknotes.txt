Abstract— Metaheuristic algorithms aim to find high
performing, near-optimal solutions with reasonable computing
costs. Using the process of natural selection that belongs to a set
of evolutionary algorithms, are Genetic Algorithms. These
algorithms intelligently exploit random search over the data in
search of a solution space for better performance. Detection of
forgery in legal documents by an automated system remains a
valid problem of today. In this paper, the identification and
detection of forgery in the monetary notes is performed by using
deep neural-based Genetic Algorithms. An Artificial Neural
Network was used for training on the banknote dataset and the
learned weights were vectorized for Genetic Algorithm
processing. Genetic Algorithm is explained in detail along with
the working of the Fitness model that determines the efficiency
of the trained model. The model achieved a high-performance
accuracy of 94% with the fitness score of 95.2. The improvement
of the model with the infusion of trainable weights from a deep
neural model is represented and analyzed.

Genetic Algorithm (GA) [9] is a stochastic search
algorithm used in machine learning that creates multiple
solutions to a given problem that evolve through a number of
generations. Each of the derived solutions from the algorithm
hold the trained weights that help optimize the output of the
model. Their adaptive heuristic approach follows an iterative
pattern that changes with time to simulate behaviors of entities
in supervised methods. The learning process of this set of
evolutionary algorithms [10] is accelerated by coupling deep
neural networks with them. The trained weights of the neural
network [11] in the hidden layers after training are converted
from weighted matrices to single dimensions vectors. These
vectors are concatenated together with selection and pooling
operations for their high performance.

I. INTRODUCTION
Counterfeit notes [1] refer to currency that is produced
without the legal sanction of the government to mimic the
original note for deceiving the recipient. The prevalence of
counterfeit notes is harmful to the economy [2] of a nation as
their circulation reduces the value of real money. Companies
also end up losing their buying power due to lack of
reimbursement of counterfeit notes. It is vital to have
automated approaches [3] to identify defective notes as high
quality counterfeits require manual examination for their
identification, which is time-consuming and infeasible on a
nation-wide scale.
There has been an extreme growth in the sheer volume of
counterfeit money in recent years with the advancement of
technology, especially with respect to production quality.
Even with more innovative security features embedded into
these notes, there have been many cases where the notes
thwarted detection. In the UK 12 million EUR worth of
counterfeit notes [4] was seized by the law enforcement
agencies earlier this year. According to the India’s National
Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), fake currency totaling
287,404 notes worth 253 million INR was seized under Fake
Indian Currency Notes (FICN) in 2019 — an increase of
11.7% over the previous year [5].
Machine Learning [6] helps in the design of systems for
the detection and identification of counterfeit notes through
feature extraction techniques. Supervised ML algorithms help
optimize performance criteria using training to solve realworld computational problems [7]. Applications of these
algorithms involved image and text processing for the
detection of counterfeit notes by the analysis of features like
watermarks, bleed lines, security thread and latent images [8].

In this paper, Genetic Algorithm modelling has been
infused with the trained weights from Artificial Neural
Networks for the identification of the counterfeit notes in the
banknote dataset. By combining the performance of the deep
neural model with the working of Genetic algorithm involving
selection of features through a fitness module for pooling.
Fitness calculator of a point aids the algorithm in to move
between points in search space for a possibly better solution.
After hyper tuning of the parameters of the implemented
model, a high classification accuracy was achieved and results
were analyzed.
The rest of the paper is organized as follows. Section II
gives the summary of some of the best works in banknote
counterfeit detection using machine learning as well as neural
modelling. The design of the system using Genetic Algorithm
to optimize neural networks is given in Section III. The
implementation details of the constructed neural model with
genetic algorithm optimization is given in Section IV. The
results obtained from the forgery detection model along with
the analysis are present in Section V. The summarization of

VI.

The dataset used in this paper is collected from [17] in the

extracted from images of both genuine and forged banknote
specimens. Wavelet transformation [18] is applied on the

III. SYSTEM DESIGN
The overall design of the GA optimized neural networks
is given in Figure 1. The weights of the neural network are
trained based on the variations introduced in the GA and a
fitness function evaluates the feasibility of the mutated
solution at each round of generation. The best solution
achieved in terms of accuracy will be assigned as the weights
of the neural network.

Image processing techniques have also been used in
machine learning algorithms to identify counterfeit notes.
Bhatia et al [15] used a fake currency classifying dataset
developed using computational and mathematical entities like
color, shape, paper width. The k-Nearest Neighbors algorithm
which was implemented for classifying the data in a suited
category using Euclidean distance to measure similarity,
outperformed the support vector classifier and gradient
boosting algorithm. k-Fold cross validation was finally
performed for the analysis of the model’s performance.
Systems for classification of counterfeit notes also used
certain security features like embedded latent image and
security thread. Singh et al [16] proposed a unique
authentication system to extract and encode these features
from Indian banknotes in image planes using a clustering
algorithm. Histogram of Oriented Gradients (HOG)
descriptor, primarily used in computer vision for identifying
edge directions, was used for encrypting the security features
using template matching and classified using the Support
Vector Machines classifier.
In summary, our model dynamically updates weights of
the neural network by applying genetic algorithm to find
weights with the best possible accuracy. Optimization is done
by applying a fitness function on the population to filter the
best possible mating pool. Offspring are generated with
features of both parents from the mating pool by applying
crossover. To improve the feature set of the offspring,
mutations are applied. Over the course of generations, the
most optimal solution is obtained. By using a deep neural
network in place of a probabilistic model like Bayes, feature
matrices are created which try to model the inputs accurately.
In the neural network, we have implemented mutation and
crossover functions on the feature set to optimize the weights
in the matrices from the input data. By using a semisupervised learning model, the initial matrices are assigned
randomly and they are optimized through training based on
the fitness function. This provides an advantage over fully
supervised models in creating a pool of population to
determine the best possible outcome from them in place of
using a single point of learning. The dataset comprises
features obtained from wavelet transformation of original
images. These features are the discrete characteristics derived
from the continuous range of inputs. In our application,

Fig. 2. Structure of Chromosomes and Genes

Genetic algorithms involve chromosomes and genes as
seen in Figure 2. Chromosomes correspond to each set of
features and genes correspond to a particular individual
feature [19]. A set of chromosomes form a population.

Fig. 3. Working of Genetic Algorithm

The first step in GA is to select the parents is in Figure 3.
Parents are individuals whose features are combined to
produce offspring. The selected parents form a mating pool
where every two parents generate two offspring. By using a
fitness function, the parents are evaluated and only those
parents with a high fitness value will be retained in the mating
pool. By iteratively applying a fitness function, only the best
solutions are retained. In order to ensure that the offspring do
not retain negative attributes of parents, variations are
introduced. They can be of the form crossover or mutations.
Crossovers introduce offspring with characteristics of both the
parents whereas mutations will change the values of the
features to improve the fitness score.

VI. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORKS
Genetic Algorithm is a Machine Learning algorithm that
generates a number of solutions over generations and
determines to arrive at a solution that gives the maximum
fitness value. The calculation of the fitness module is done to
determine the best possible mating pool of parents using the
trained matrix weights, vectorized from the learned weights
from the training of the Deep Neural network. A dataset
containing the detection of counterfeit notes was used to study
the working of the implement deep neural Genetic Algorithm
and the evaluation of the model is done over a number of
performance metrics. The deep neural Genetic Algorithm
guarantees highly optimized solutions for any complex dataset
or datasets with less trainable features, like in this case. The
feature selection performed through selection of solutions
could be optimized to explore the applications of the model in
hyperparameter tuning of trained deep neural networks to
build robust systems.
